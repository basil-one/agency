## When This Makes Sense

Maybe you've started using AI for more than getting things done.

You're thinking with it: exploring ideas, working through decisions, preparing for difficult conversations, and considering different perspectives.

The conversation moves. The responses sound convincing. New possibilities keep emerging.

**Yet somehow, what you're trying to understand isn't coming into clearer focus.**

What if another lens could help you see something you've been missing?

We all bring patterns into how we think, interpret situations, interact with others, and engage with AI. Some serve us well. Others can limit what we notice or how we respond, particularly under pressure.

*Know Your Patterns* is for people curious about developing their own capabilities alongside AI. It's not simply about becoming better at using the technology, but becoming more aware of what they bring to the interaction.

</section>

<section id="experience" markdown="1">

## What You'll Experience

### Discover another lens

Before we meet, you'll complete the Focus Energy Balance Indicator (FEBI®), an assessment that helps illuminate patterns in how you direct your energy, engage with situations, and respond under pressure.

You'll receive your personal report and have time to begin reflecting on what stands out.

### Explore what it reveals

In our private coaching session, we'll connect the lens to situations that matter to you.

You bring your experience. I bring questions, perspective, and a framework for reflection. Together, we'll explore distinctions, connections, and possibilities that may not have been apparent before.

### Bring the lens into your work with AI

We'll explore practical ways to reason with the lens using AI. This might include examining situations from different perspectives, questioning assumptions, or considering responses that might not come naturally.

I'll also share approaches I've found useful in my own AI-assisted reflection and reasoning. You may find some worth considering or experimenting with in your own way.

The lens becomes something you can continue working with, not simply a report you receive.

</section>
