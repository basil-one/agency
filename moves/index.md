# Agency Moves

Agency Moves are in development, with publication planned for 2027.

The moves will explore practical ways to recognize patterns, navigate difficult situations, and choose how to respond.

When published, original Agency Moves will be shared under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (CC BY-SA 4.0).
