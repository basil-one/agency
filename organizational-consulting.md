# Organizational Consulting

When organizational change stalls, the difficulty isn't always a missing plan or process. People can agree on the goal and still understand the situation differently, encounter different impediments, or struggle to move together.

I work at the pattern level: recognizing recurring problems across contexts, helping people surface what is getting in the way, and creating conditions for shared understanding and coordinated action. The work starts with the situation people are actually navigating, rather than assuming a particular method is the answer.

## An example: Circle3

[Circle3](https://circle.basil.one) is one published example of this approach. It offers three guiding moves for leading difficult change through a small group and recurring, facilitated conversations. It is an illustration of the kind of pattern-based work I develop and apply—not the only form an engagement can take.

[Read the Circle3 paper](https://circle.basil.one/paper/)

## Working together

A useful engagement needs to work for the organization, any sourcing partner, and the practitioner. Specialized consultants often bring independent research, published approaches, and professional communities that extend beyond a single contract.

The [Pattern-Level Consultant — Mutual Advantage Model]({{ '/pattern-level-consultant/' | relative_url }}) offers considerations for discussing those relationships, including intellectual property, independent practice, sponsorship, and engagement structures. It is a resource for conversations about fit and terms, not a required package.
